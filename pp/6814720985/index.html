VPN, Proxy and Network Data

The Application uses Apple's Network Extension framework to create a local VPN tunnel on Your Device. The tunnel routes Your network traffic according to the proxy configurations, rules and servers that You import or create yourself. The Company does not operate these proxy servers; traffic sent through them is subject to the policies of whoever operates them.

We do not log, collect, store, sell, or share Your browsing activity. This includes the websites and domains You visit, DNS queries, destination IP addresses, connection content, and traffic volume. None of this information is transmitted to the Company.

Proxy configuration files, rule sets, server credentials that You enter, and the connection and request logs shown in the Log Analyzer are created and stored only on Your Device. They are processed locally so that You can inspect and troubleshoot Your own network traffic. You can clear logs or delete configurations at any time within the Application, and all of this data is removed when You delete the Application.

The Application does not use network data for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
